How it sounds like speech

Every vowel group in your text becomes one syllable. A buzzing source is shaped by four resonant filters (formants) set to measured vowel frequencies, so “moon” and “see” sound different. The size knob scales those formants together, which is why an orc sounds big and not just low. Punctuation gives the melody: pitch drifts down across a phrase, rises on a question, and pauses at commas and full stops. Small random wobbles in pitch keep it from sounding like a machine.
